# Privacy Policy for Rush Office

**Last updated:** February 2026

Alcore Studio built the **Rush Office** game as a free-to-play mobile game. This game is provided at no cost and is intended for use as is.

This Privacy Policy is meant to inform users regarding our policies on the collection, use, and disclosure of information if anyone decides to use our game.

---

## Information Collection and Use

Rush Office **does not collect, store, or require users to provide any personally identifiable information** such as name, email address, phone number, or physical address.

However, the app uses **third-party services** that may collect information used to identify your device for analytics, advertising, and gameplay-related services.

---

## Third-Party Services

Rush Office uses the following third-party SDKs and services:

- **Unity LevelPlay (IronSource / Unity Ads mediation)** – for serving ads
- **Google AdMob** – ad mediation partner
- **Google Play Services** – achievements, leaderboards, and saved games

These services may collect:
- Device or other unique identifiers (such as Advertising ID)
- Approximate device information (OS version, device model)
- Ad interaction data (impressions, clicks)

You can review their privacy policies here:

- Unity Privacy Policy: https://unity.com/legal/privacy-policy
- Google Privacy Policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy

---

## Advertising

Rush Office displays advertisements to support development and maintenance of the game.

Third-party ad providers may use Advertising IDs to serve personalized or non-personalized ads. Users can opt out of personalized advertising through their device settings.

---

## Data Security

We value your trust in providing a safe experience. All data transmitted by third-party SDKs is **encrypted in transit** using industry-standard security measures.

The developer does not maintain its own servers for collecting or storing user data.

---

## Children’s Privacy

Rush Office does not knowingly collect personally identifiable information from children under the age of 13.

If you are a parent or guardian and believe your child has provided personal information, please contact us so that we can take appropriate action.

---

## Changes to This Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated from time to time. Users are advised to review this page periodically for any changes.

Changes are effective immediately after they are posted on this page.
